# Hungarian government supported home loans

Status date: 2026-10-13. Checked against the consolidated decree texts on net.jogtar.hu (518/2023., 227/2025. and 302/2023. as of 2026-03-06, 44/2019. as of 2026-06-30, 337/2017. as of 2025-01-10), then mnb.hu, csalad.hu, kormany.hu and bank and comparison sites. Items marked (?) are not fully confirmed.

## Political context

- A Tisza-led government took office after the April 2026 election. It keeps the housing supports but plans to "reshape" them (Finance Ministry, Sept 2026)
- No amendment to 227/2025., 518/2023., 44/2019., 302/2023. or 337/2017. was published up to Magyar Közlöny 2026/151 (2026-10-09)
- Otthon Start: under review (Finance Ministry, 2026-09-10), nothing published for 2027. An upper age limit (35-40), a rental ban and a lower ownership share are analyst expectations, not decisions. The "kiemelt beruházás" status of Otthon Start developments (335/2025.) is suspended until 2026-12-31, which affects builders, not borrowers
- Babaváró: 101/2026. moved deadlines falling between 2024-07-01 and 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-01 (38. §). The decision on what follows was still pending on 2026-09-30. It concerns existing contracts only, new loans keep the 5 year deadline

## Combinations

- No decree caps the number of subsidized loans. The "at most two" press line follows from the pairs: CSOK Plusz and the Falusi CSOK-kölcsön exclude each other (302/2023. 53. § (15)), Otthon Start goes with either (227/2025. 1. § (3), 9. § (5) c); 518/2023. 23. § (3))
- The Falusi CSOK grant goes with all of them, Babaváró is not a housing loan and goes with everything

## Otthon Start

- Legal basis: 227/2025. (VII. 31.) Korm. rendelet, amended by 332/2025. (külterület from 2026-01-01) and 40/2026. (építményi jog)
- Interest subsidized loan, fixed for the full term. Purchase or construction only, not refinancing or renovation
- TB (8. § (1) c)): 2 years continuous (30 day gaps allowed), the last 180 days in an employment-type relationship. No shorter rule for the self-employed. For spouses one meeting the TB and ownership rules is enough (4. § (5a))
- Price may differ from the bank's valuation by at most 20% either way (6. § (6)). Apply within 180 days of the contract, 5 year state mortgage, one loan per person for life, seller cannot be a close relative
- First home: no home owned in the last 10 years, except one under 15M, a share of at most 50%, demolished, usufruct-encumbered or inherited property
- Penalty on breach: subsidy repaid at jegybanki alapkamat + 5 pp. Early repayment fee max 1%

## CSOK Plusz

- Legal basis: 518/2023. (XI. 30.) Korm. rendelet, last amended by 40/2026.
- Married couples, at least one new promised child. Existing children count toward the amount and floor area
- Wife under 41, no limit from 12 weeks of pregnancy or during adoption (permanent from 2026-01-01)
- TB (15. § (1) c)): one spouse, 2 years (1 year for átalányadózó or KATA), the last 180 days in an employment-type relationship
- No minimum amount in the decree (11. § has caps only), banks set 0.5M (OTP) to 2M. Minimum term 10 years (12. § (1)), the subsidy runs at most 25 years
- First year interest only: principal is repaid from the 13th month (12. § (2))
- Child deadline (19. §) belongs to the number of promised children, from the loan contract (purchase) or the final occupancy permit (construction, expansion). Pregnancy at the deadline extends it to the birth, adoption by 2 years
- Forgiveness (46. § (1)) counts only children born after the application: the 2nd such child and each later one. The 1st such child gives a 12 month pause (43. §), at the earliest from the 13th month (45. § (2)). During the pause the borrower's interest accrues outside the principal and is repaid in yearly parts afterwards, with a longer term so the installment does not grow (44. § (5)-(6))
- Partial fulfilment (37. § (1)): only the loan part above what the born children justify loses the subsidy. That part of the subsidy is repaid with jegybanki alapkamat + 5 pp interest within 120 days
- Other home case (6. § (8) b)): the price must exceed the value of the home the couple owns, or of the one they sold in the last 2 years. Buying back a home sold within 5 years is barred. 10 years residence, max 10% of the price in cash, once per couple

## Babaváró

- Legal basis: 44/2019. (III. 12.) Korm. rendelet, amended by 437/2024. (wife's age), 388/2024., 282/2025. and 101/2026. (deadline extension)
- Interest free personal loan, free to use, counts in JTM. As önerő it depends on the bank
- Guarantee fee 0.5% a year on the balance, part of the 51k cap, not charged during a pause (18. § (1), (1a))
- TB (4. § (1) c)): one spouse, 3 years (1 year for átalányadózó or KATA). The reported 1 year / 50% income relief from 2026-10-01 does not exist
- A child counts from the 12th week of pregnancy. No child within 5 years (15. §): the rate becomes at most ÁKK 5 year yield x 130% + 4 pp (5.28% for October 2026, so 10.86%, OTP), and within 120 days the borrower pays jegybanki alapkamat + 3 pp interest on the disbursed amount for the period (or the subsidy received if higher). Alapkamat: 5.50% since 2026-08-26. A later child lets the couple reapply for the subsidy going forward (15. § (11))

## Falusi CSOK and Falusi CSOK-kölcsön

- Legal basis: 302/2023. (VII. 11.) Korm. rendelet, amended by 40/2026.
- Who (2. § (2), 11. §): married couples and partners, and single parents with their existing children. Only a married couple with the wife under 41 may promise children, deadlines 4 / 8 / 10 years (20. § (3)-(4))
- TB (15. § (1) c)): one applicant, 2 years (1 year for átalányadózó or KATA)
- About 2,630 preferált kistelepülés plus külterület tanya and birtokközpont, listed in the decree's annex. Register residence within 180 days and live there 10 years (15. § (7))
- Used home: must come with modernization or expansion, at most half the grant toward the price (12. § (2)). Modernization or expansion alone: 600k / 2M / 7.5M for 1 / 2 / 3+ children (12. § (3)), finished within 4 years
- Kölcsön (53. §): only with the 2 or 3+ child grant, the borrower pays at most 3%, subsidy up to 25 years. Purchase, construction, modernization and expansion. Goes with Otthon Start, not with CSOK Plusz
- Children born or adopted later and not counted in the grant (23. §): 1M each, prepaying a bank housing loan without a fee, claimed at the bank. A fulfilled promise does not count. It does not exclude jelzáloghitel-elengedés for the same child

## Jelzáloghitel-elengedés

- Legal basis: 337/2017. (XI. 14.) Korm. rendelet
- Any forint housing mortgage, Otthon Start and CSOK Plusz included, never Babaváró. The loan application must precede the birth (4. § (1)); a child already on the way when applying counts, and can be claimed from the 12th week
- Child order counts every child in the household (6. § (6)-(7)). Never more than the remaining debt
- One benefit per child: either the CSOK Plusz forgiveness or this one (4. § (4), 518/2023. 47. § (8))
- At least 50% ownership, no co-debtor besides the spouse or partner. Apply until 1 year after the birth (csalad.hu)

## Stamp duty

- Legal basis: 1990. évi XCIII. törvény (Itv.), version in force since 2026-10-01. No housing duty change announced for 2027 as of 2026-10-13
- General rate (19. § (1)): 4% up to 1 billion HUF per property, 2% above, capped at 200M
- CSOK Plusz or Falusi CSOK grant (26. § (1a) f)): the whole purchase is exempt, even when Otthon Start finances the rest. Repaying the support in full later brings the duty back (26. § (2g)). Otthon Start and the Falusi CSOK-kölcsön alone get nothing
- New home from a developer (26. § (1) f)): the 15M / 30M test uses the market value, no support is deducted. Not available when the buyer proves selling another home (lakáscsere applies instead)
- Under 35, first home (26. § (6)): up to 50% off when the whole home is worth at most 15M. Age at the contract, shares are proportional (one qualifying spouse of two: 25%). First home (26. § (9)): never owned a home or a share of at least 50%, relief not used before
- Lakáscsere: dearer new home pays on the price difference if the old one was sold within 5 years before (3 years until 2025-12-31) or 1 year after (21. § (2) b)). Cheaper new home is exempt with a 3 year / 1 year window (26. § (1) y))

## MNB rules (adósságfék)

- Legal basis: 32/2014. (IX. 10.) MNB rendelet, last relevant amendment 3/2026. (II. 26.)
- JTM (6. § (3)-(4)) applies to mortgages longer than 5 years, by the shortest fixation. Income threshold 600k until 2025-12-31, 800k from 2026-01-01 (28/2025. MNB rendelet)
- Debt service and net income of all co-debtors are summed, every existing installment counts, Babaváró included (5. § (1)-(2)). Subsidized loans count at their actual installment (5. § (9))
- First-time buyer for 90% LTV (3. § (1a)): no borrower ever owned a share of at least 50% of a home. No age limit since 2025-09-02
- Green purpose (energy class A+ and at most 68 kWh/m²/year, or a renovation reaching A or saving 30%): 90% LTV when the green home is the collateral and 60% JTM regardless of income with at least 10 years fixation (3. § (1c), 6. § (4a)). Applies to Otthon Start and CSOK Plusz too, they are ordinary forint mortgages
- Green products in 2026 (money.hu, 2026-10-08): CIB, MBH, Erste, K&H, UniCredit, OTP, usually 0.25-0.5 pp below the normal rate
- Loans of at most 550k are outside the rules (1. § (3) a))

## Bank rates

- No Hungarian bank has a public mortgage API: developer portals are PSD2 only (accounts and payments, licence and eIDAS certificate needed)
- Comparison sites are licensed intermediaries with their own database, updated from the banks' lakáshitel hirdetmény, behind a private backend
- Market average: ECB MIR series `MIR.M.HU.B.A2C.A.R.A.2250.HUF.N` (Hungarian new housing loans, monthly, about 6 weeks lag)
