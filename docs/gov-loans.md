# Hungarian government-supported home loans and housing subsidies

Status date: 2026-10-04. Compiled from official legal texts (net.jogtar.hu mirrors of Korm. rendelet texts), mnb.hu, csalad.hu, kormany.hu, allamkincstar.gov.hu, bank pages and major comparison sites (bankmonitor.hu, bank360.hu, money.hu). Items marked (?) are not fully confirmed - see "Uncertain / needs verification".

## Political context (important for 2026)

- After the April 2026 election a Tisza-led government took office. It has said it keeps the housing supports but will "reshape" them (Finance Minister Kármán András, State Secretary Boda Nikoletta, Sept 2026).
- Otthon Start: a legal and professional review is in progress. Discussed (not decided): an upper age limit (35-40), lowering the allowed prior ownership share from 50% to 49% or less, banning rental or investment use. Market expectation: new rules from 2027-01-01. As of 2026-10-04 no amending decree has been published.
- Otthon Start "kiemelt beruházás" (priority investment) status for related developments is suspended until 2026-12-31 (Vitézy Dávid, Aug 2026). This affects builders, not borrowers.
- Babaváró: the extended child deadline expires 2026-11-01. The Finance Ministry says it will decide on a solution before then (about 24,700 contracts, 182 bn HUF).
- New: Wekerle Sándor Lakásépítési Program (1275/2026. (VIII. 31.) Korm. határozat) - supply side (rental housing, dormitories, brownfield), not a household loan.
- 5% VAT on new homes: transitional rule to 2030 confirmed by the government (Sept 2026).

## 1. Otthon Start - FIX 3%-os lakáshitel

- EN: "Home Start" fixed 3% first-home loan.
- Status: open since 2025-09-01. No end date in the decree. Review in progress, tightening expected from 2027 (not decided).
- Legal basis: 227/2025. (VII. 31.) Korm. rendelet. Amended by 332/2025. (X. 30.) Korm. rendelet (eg. külterület allowed from 2026-01-01) and 40/2026. (III. 5.) Korm. rendelet (építményi jog / pre-occupancy financing, from 2026-03-06).
- Type: interest-subsidized loan (state pays monthly kamattámogatás to the bank).
- Max amount: 50,000,000 HUF. OTP minimum 500,000 HUF. Amount does not depend on children.
- Interest: max 3% fixed for the whole term. THM about 3.1-3.3% at banks. Early repayment fee max 1% of the prepaid amount.
- Term: max 25 years (OTP: 6-25 years). No statutory grace period found.
- Purpose: purchase or construction of a home (lakás, egylakásos lakóépület, tanya, birtokközpont). Not for refinancing, not for renovation-only or expansion-only.
- Eligibility:
  - Age: 18+. No upper age limit (as of now).
  - Marriage: not required. Single applicants or married couples. Co-debtors limited to spouse, parent, sibling (parents/siblings cannot become owners).
  - Children: none required, no child commitment.
  - TB jogviszony: 2 years continuous before application (for couples, one spouse is enough), plus qualifying employment in the last 180 days (public work and unemployment benefit do not count; GYED/CSED and some others accepted).
  - Criminal record: büntetlen előélet required.
  - Public debt: max about 5,000 HUF (465/2017. Korm. rendelet threshold).
  - First-home rule: no residential property owned in the previous 10 years, except: property worth under 15,000,000 HUF; max 50% share in one property; demolished property; usufruct-encumbered property where the usufructuary still lives there; inherited shares (spouses may buy out the rest). Up to 180 days overlap allowed for sale and purchase.
  - Seller cannot be a close relative.
  - Lifetime limit: one Otthon Start loan per person, even after repayment.
  - Income: no statutory cap; normal bank credit check plus MNB JTM.
- Property requirements:
  - Max value: apartment in multi-unit building 100,000,000 HUF; single-family house, tanya, birtokközpont 150,000,000 HUF.
  - Max price per m2: 1,500,000 HUF (gross).
  - Purchase price may exceed bank valuation by max 20%.
  - New or used. Since 2026-01-01 külterület residential buildings also allowed (zártkert only if a real residential building).
  - When combined with CSOK Plusz, the CSOK Plusz minimum floor area rules apply.
  - No energy class requirement found.
- Önerő / LTV: min 10% önerő (decree), unless extra collateral. Banks apply MNB HFM (90% for first-time buyers) and JTM. In practice banks often require more.
- Deadlines: purchase - apply within 180 days of the sales contract; construction - before the occupancy permit. Construction must start within 1 year and finish within 6 years; new-build purchase completion within 3 years of first disbursement.
- State security: state mortgage and alienation ban for 5 years after completion (securing 20% of the loan).
- Child events: none built in. Jelzáloghitel-elengedés (section 8) can be claimed on an Otthon Start loan for the 2nd and later children.
- Sanctions: false statements, demolition, sale or business use during the state mortgage period end the subsidy; repayment of subsidy with interest at jegybanki alapkamat + 5 percentage points.
- Rental: the draft ban was removed from the final decree. Renting appears allowed now; a ban is under discussion for 2027.
- Combinable with: CSOK Plusz (same property), Falusi CSOK grant, ÁFA-visszatérítés, Babaváró (as önerő, bank-dependent), jelzáloghitel-elengedés, Otthontámogatás, employer support. Illeték: no exemption on its own (4%), but exempt if combined with CSOK Plusz or Falusi CSOK.
- Banks: all major banks - OTP, MBH, K&H, Erste, Raiffeisen, CIB, UniCredit, Gránit, MagNet (?).

## 2. CSOK Plusz

- EN: Family home-creation preferential loan "CSOK Plus".
- Status: open (since 2024-01-01). No program end date found in current texts (?). Age exception made permanent from 2026-01-01.
- Legal basis: 518/2023. (XI. 30.) Korm. rendelet a családok otthonteremtését támogató kedvezményes CSOK Plusz hitelprogramról (last amended by 40/2026. (III. 5.)).
- Type: interest-subsidized loan + state forgiveness per child.
- Max amount (depends on existing + vállalt children):
  - 1 child (childless couple promising 1): 15,000,000 HUF
  - 2 children (promise 2, or 1 existing + 1 promised): 30,000,000 HUF
  - 3+ children (or equivalent existing + promised): 50,000,000 HUF
  - Minimum about 2,500,000 HUF (?).
- Interest: max 3% fixed for borrower, state subsidy for the whole term while conditions hold. Bank reference rate in decree: 5-year ÁKK average yield x 110% + 1 pp.
- Term: 10-25 years (bank sources). First 12 months: principal-free / interest-only reported by money.hu (?).
- Eligibility:
  - Marriage: married couples only.
  - Age: wife under 41 at application. Exception (permanent from 2026-01-01): any age if at least 12-week pregnancy or ongoing adoption procedure.
  - Children: at least 1 new vállalt child. Existing children count for amount and floor area.
  - TB: one spouse 2 years continuous (30-day gaps allowed) + 180 days of qualifying activity.
  - Criminal record: both spouses clean. Public debt max 5,000 HUF.
  - Ownership: "first home" (első otthon) or "larger home" (nagyobb otthon) case. In the larger-home case the new home must be worth more than the previously co-owned home (?details on sale obligation).
  - Can be used only once per couple.
- Property requirements:
  - Max value: first home 100,000,000 HUF (apartment) / 150,000,000 HUF (single-family house); other cases 150,000,000 HUF. Older sources quote 80,000,000 HUF for first home (pre-2025 rule).
  - Purchase price max 20% above valuation.
  - Min useful floor area: 1 child 40 m2, 2 children 50 m2, 3+ children 60 m2 for apartments; +30 m2 for single-family houses (70/80/90 m2).
  - New or used purchase, construction, expansion (min 12 m2 room). Not for modernization only or land only. Hungary only.
  - Residence: 10 years as main residence; move in within 180 days.
  - Max 10% of price in cash.
- Önerő / LTV: per MNB HFM (80%, 90% for first-time buyers). Babaváró may be used as önerő (bank-dependent).
- Child commitment deadlines (from contract or occupancy permit): 1 child 4 years, 2 children 8 years, 3 children 10 years. Pregnancy at deadline (reported within 90 days) extends to birth; adoption can extend by 2 years.
- Child events:
  - 1st child born after contract: 12-month repayment suspension on request.
  - 2nd and each further child born after the application: 10,000,000 HUF forgiveness each (state pays principal). Children born before the application do not count for forgiveness.
  - Report birth within 90 days.
- Promised children not born: kamattámogatás must be repaid with interest at jegybanki alapkamat + 5 pp; loan becomes non-subsidized. Partial fulfilment rules (?).
- Combinable with: Otthon Start (same property, both applied at once, up to 50M + 50M), Falusi CSOK grant (same bank, same time), Babaváró, ÁFA-visszatérítés, illetékmentesség (automatic), employer support, Otthontámogatás. Not with Falusi CSOK-kölcsön. Jelzáloghitel-elengedés and CSOK Plusz forgiveness cannot both be claimed for the same child (?).
- Banks: CIB, Erste, Gránit, K&H, MagNet, MBH, OTP, Raiffeisen, UniCredit.

## 3. Babaváró támogatás (Babaváró hitel)

- EN: "Baby-expecting" interest-free family loan.
- Status: open. Application end date removed (no deadline in the decree). Extended first-child deadline ends 2026-11-01; government decision pending.
- Legal basis: 44/2019. (III. 12.) Korm. rendelet (amended by 437/2024., 388/2024., 282/2025., 101/2026. and the June 2026 Tisza-government extension in Magyar Közlöny 2026/81).
- Type: interest-free (state-subsidized) personal loan + forgiveness. Not a housing loan; freely usable.
- Max amount: 11,000,000 HUF (since 2024-01-01).
- Monthly installment: max 51,000 HUF (incl. guarantee fee).
- Interest: 0% while conditions hold. If support ends: ÁKK reference yield x 130% + 4 pp (max).
- Term: 5-20 years (suspensions extend it).
- Eligibility:
  - Marriage required; at least one spouse Hungarian address/citizenship rules.
  - Wife age: 18 to under 35 at application (from 2025-01-01, 437/2024.). Husband no limit.
  - TB: one spouse 3 years continuous (1 year for átalányadózó or KATA self-employed). Reports of a 1-year TB / 50% income relief from 2026-10-01 are not confirmed (?).
  - Criminal record clean, no public debt.
- Child events:
  - 1st child: loan stays interest-free for the whole term, 3-year suspension.
  - 2nd child: 30% of remaining debt forgiven + 3-year suspension.
  - 3rd child: remaining debt fully forgiven.
  - Child counts from 12-week pregnancy; deadline for first child 5 years from disbursement.
- No child within 5 years: subsidy ends, accrued kamattámogatás repaid as lump sum within 120 days, loan becomes interest-bearing (ÁKK x 130% + 4 pp). Special extension: deadlines falling 2024-07-01 to 2026-10-31 moved to 2026-11-01. Divorce without child ends support.
- Combinable with: everything (used as önerő for Otthon Start / CSOK Plusz is bank-dependent; it counts in JTM).
- Banks: OTP, K&H, Erste, Gránit, CIB, Raiffeisen, UniCredit, MBH.

## 4. Falusi CSOK (falusi otthonteremtési támogatás) and Falusi CSOK-kölcsön

- EN: Rural family home-creation grant (+ subsidized rural loan).
- Status: open since 2019-07-01, no end date ("visszavonásig"). No Tisza-government change published by end Sept 2026.
- Legal basis: 302/2023. (VII. 11.) Korm. rendelet a kistelepüléseken nyújtható otthonteremtési támogatásokról (amended by 40/2026. (III. 5.)).
- Type: non-repayable grant; optional subsidized loan.
- Grant amounts:

| Children | New build / new purchase / used purchase + modernization | Modernization / expansion only |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1        | 1,000,000 HUF                                            | 600,000 HUF                    |
| 2        | 4,000,000 HUF                                            | 2,000,000 HUF                  |
| 3+       | 15,000,000 HUF                                           | 7,500,000 HUF                  |

- Used purchase: max 50% of the grant toward the price, rest toward modernization/expansion.
- Falusi CSOK-kölcsön: max 10,000,000 HUF (2 children) or 15,000,000 HUF (3+ children), interest max 3%, state-subsidized. Term max 25 years (?). Cannot be combined with CSOK Plusz.
- Eligibility: married couples (csalad.hu); single parents with existing children may be eligible for parts (?). Wife under 41 for vállalt children. 2 years TB for one spouse (1 year for some self-employed). Clean criminal record, no public debt. At least 1 existing or promised child.
- Property / location: about 2,630 preferált kistelepülés (mostly under 5,000 inhabitants, population-loss criteria) plus all külterület tanya / birtokközpont residences. Min useful area: 40/50/60 m2 apartment, 70/80/90 m2 house for 1/2/3+ children.
- Residence: 10 years (grantis) - older rule was 5 years (?). Move in within 180 days. Modernization must finish within 4 years (?).
- Child deadlines: same pattern as CSOK Plusz (4 / 8 years) (?).
- Non-fulfilment: grant repaid with penalty interest (statutory rate + 3 pp per grantis) (?).
- Combinable with: CSOK Plusz (same property, same bank, same time), Otthon Start, Babaváró, ÁFA-visszatérítés, illetékmentesség.
- Banks: OTP, MBH, Erste, K&H (product-dependent); CSOK Plusz banks generally.

## 5. ÁFA-visszatérítési támogatás (preferált kistelepülés) and 5% áfa on new homes

### 5a. ÁFA-visszatérítés (VAT refund grant)

- Status: open (?). Some sources say the preferential rule runs to 2026-12-31.
- Legal basis: 302/2023. (VII. 11.) Korm. rendelet.
- Type: grant equal to VAT paid.
- Variants (each max 5,000,000 HUF, once; earlier refunds deducted):
  - New home purchase/build with 5% VAT: requires Falusi CSOK; max 300 m2 useful area.
  - Self-build (27% VAT on materials/services): own plot, preferált kistelepülés; claimed with Falusi CSOK / CSOK Plusz.
  - Modernization/expansion (27% VAT): owners in preferált kistelepülés.
- Location: only preferált kistelepülés and külterület (same list as Falusi CSOK) in 2026.

### 5b. 5% lakásáfa (reduced VAT on new homes)

- Status: 5% applies to new homes whose building permit becomes final (or simple notification acknowledged) by 2026-12-31, if delivered/paid by 2030-12-31. Brownfield (rozsdaövezet) homes keep 5% beyond 2027. After 2026-12-31 new permits: 27% unless the autumn 2026 tax package changes it (?).
- Size limits: apartment max 150 m2, house max 300 m2 useful area.
- Legal basis: Áfa tv. (2007. évi CXXVII.) transitional provisions.

## 6. Illetékmentesség and illetékkedvezmény (stamp duty)

- Legal basis: 1990. évi XCIII. törvény (Itv.). General rate 4% of market value (Itv. 27. §).
- CSOK Plusz or Falusi CSOK purchase: full exemption regardless of value (must be stated in the contract).
- Otthon Start alone: no exemption (4%); with CSOK Plusz: exempt.
- New home from developer: exempt up to 15,000,000 HUF if value (reduced by non-repayable state support) is max 30,000,000 HUF; 4% on the part above 15M. Above 30M: full 4%.
- Under 35, first home: 50% reduction if value max 15,000,000 HUF (Itv. 26. § - exact point ?); installment payment up to 12 months.
- Lakáscsere: base reduced by the sale price of the previous home sold within 3 years before or 1 year after the purchase (?; one source says 5 years).
- Building plot: exempt if a home with occupancy permit is built within 4 years.

## 7. Legacy CSOK (Családi Otthonteremtési Kedvezmény, 2015-2023)

- Status: closed for new claims since 2023-12-31 (new home: 16/2016. (II. 10.), used home: 17/2016. (II. 10.) Korm. rendelet; CSOK kamattámogatott hitel 10M closed too). Only Falusi CSOK survives as a non-repayable CSOK.
- Still relevant: existing commitments. If promised children are not born (1 child 4 years, 2 children 8 years, 3 children 10 years) the share of the grant for each unborn child is repaid with interest within 120 days. Hardship relief exists (eg. fertility treatment).
- Interaction: former CSOK recipients can take CSOK Plusz, but unfulfilled commitments must be settled (?).

## 8. Jelzáloghitel-elengedés (nagycsaládosok jelzáloghitel-tartozás csökkentése)

- EN: Mortgage debt reduction for families with 2+ children.
- Status: open, unchanged in 2026.
- Legal basis: 337/2017. (XI. 14.) Korm. rendelet a három- vagy többgyermekes családok lakáscélú jelzáloghitel-tartozásainak csökkentéséről.
- Type: forgiveness (state pays the bank).
- Amounts: 2nd child 1,000,000 HUF; 3rd child 4,000,000 HUF; each further child 1,000,000 HUF. Children born from 2018-01-01 (3rd+) and 2019-07-01 (2nd).
- Eligible loans: housing-purpose mortgage (purchase, build, expand, modernize) incl. Otthon Start, CSOK-related mortgages, housing leasing, employer housing loans; contracts before 2017-11-14 any mortgage on a Hungarian home. The loan must be contracted before the birth (or 12-week pregnancy). Not Babaváró.
- Conditions: clean criminal record, no public debt, at least 50% ownership of one parent, joint application by spouses/partners.
- Deadline: from 12th week of pregnancy until 1 year after birth / final adoption (csalad.hu). One source says 60 days (?). Apply at kormányablak / járási hivatal.
- CSOK Plusz: cannot claim both for the same child on the CSOK Plusz loan (?).

## 9. Otthonfelújítási programs

- Otthonfelújítási támogatás (50% up to 3M grant) and otthonfelújítási kölcsön (6M, 0%): closed 2022-12-31.
- Vidéki Otthonfelújítási Program (families with children under 25 and pensioners in preferált kistelepülés, 50% up to 3,000,000 HUF grant): closed 2026-06-30, not extended.
- Otthonfelújítási Program (energetic, pre-2007 family houses, min 30% primary energy saving): up to 5,000,000 HUF grant + up to 5,000,000 HUF 0% loan (max 15 years), 5% own contribution. Funds exhausted, suspended nationwide from 2026-04-23. The ministry (Kapitány István) is working on paying out about 34.6 bn HUF excess demand; a new energy program (Családi Energiaprogram, about 100,000 homes/year) was promised but not launched as of 2026-10-04.

## 10. Munkáshitel (not housing)

- EN: Young workers' interest-free loan.
- Status: open since 2025-01-01, no end date.
- Legal basis: Korm. rendelet from late 2024 (number ?).
- Max 4,000,000 HUF, 0% interest, term 2-10 years.
- Eligibility: age 17 to under 25/26 (?), 3 months continuous TB, min 20 h/week work, no higher-education student status or degree, clean record, public debt max 5,000 HUF, commit to 5 years Hungarian residence and work.
- Child events (women): 1st child 2-year suspension; 2nd child further 2 years + 50% forgiveness; 3rd child full forgiveness.
- Breach: repay subsidy within 120 days; rate ÁKK x 130% + 4 pp.
- Housing relevance: freely usable; counts in JTM. Use as önerő is bank-dependent (?).
- Banks: OTP, MBH, K&H, Erste, CIB, Raiffeisen.

## 11. MNB Zöld Otthon Program

- Closed. MNB refinancing program for green new homes (max 2.5% fixed) ran 2021-2022; allocation exhausted in 2022.
- What remains: MNB JTM allows 60% for green-purpose HUF loans regardless of income (see MNB rules). Banks offer their own green mortgages (not state-subsidized).

## 12. Munkáltatói lakáscélú támogatás (employer housing support)

- Legal basis: Szja tv. (1995. évi CXVII.) 1. számú melléklet (lakáscélú támogatás point ?) + 15/2014. (III. 13.) NGM rendelet.
- Type: tax-free employer grant (voluntary for employer).
- Limits: max 30% of price / construction / modernization cost, and max 5,000,000 HUF from all employers within the tax year and previous 4 years. Can also be paid toward repayment of a housing loan.
- Combinable with all programs.

## 13. Fiatalok lakhatási támogatása (employer benefit, under 35)

- Legal basis: Szja tv. amendment + 403/2024. (XII. 18.) Korm. rendelet (?).
- Status: open (from 2025-01-01 per government announcement; one source says 2026 ?).
- Up to 150,000 HUF/month (1,800,000 HUF/year) for rent or housing loan installments; employer pays 15% SZJA + 13% szocho (28%). Voluntary for employer. Age under 35.

## 14. Otthontámogatás (közszolgálati dolgozók)

- Legal basis: 361/2025. (XI. 25.) Korm. rendelet az Otthontámogatásról (amended early 2026).
- Type: grant, net 1,000,000 HUF/year per eligible public-sector employee (2,000,000 HUF if both spouses eligible). For existing housing loan repayment or önerő of a new loan (bank loan, leasing, LTP loan, employer loan).
- 2026 round: declaration deadline 2026-02-15, payment by 2026-03-30. From 2027 annual declaration, only if funded in the annual budget (Tisza-government stance unknown ?).
- Combinable with Otthon Start, CSOK Plusz, etc.

## 15. Lakástakarékpénztár

- State subsidy (30%, max 72,000 HUF/year) ended for new contracts in Oct 2018. LTPs still exist with bank-paid bonuses; savings usable as önerő (since Nov 2024 also holiday homes). No state-backed "Otthonteremtési megtakarítás" program found for 2026.

## 16. SZJA (income tax) rules affecting affordability

- 25 év alattiak SZJA-mentessége (up to average wage) - continues in 2026.
- Anyák SZJA-mentessége: 4+ children (since 2020), under-30 mothers (since 2023), 3-children mothers no age limit (since 2025-10-01), 2-children mothers under 40 (from 2026-01-01), under 50 (2027), under 60 (2028), all (2029) - later steps depend on the new government.
- Effect: higher net income raises JTM capacity. No first-home-buyer SZJA allowance exists.

- CSOK Plusz + Falusi CSOK-kölcsön: mutually exclusive (csalad.hu).
- Babaváró as önerő: accepted by many banks but it is a loan, so it counts in JTM (bankmonitor, grantis).
- Jelzáloghitel-elengedés on Otthon Start: allowed (Otthon Start is a housing mortgage; hiteltudakozo.hu, biztosdontes.hu). Not for Babaváró (bank360).
- Illeték: CSOK Plusz or Falusi CSOK makes the whole purchase exempt even if Otthon Start finances the rest.
- Some sources claim a family can hold max 2 state-subsidized loans at once (?).

## Purpose of the money (what each source may pay for)

| Source              | Purchase / build  | Renovation / modernization           | Free use (keep, invest) |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Otthon Start        | Y                 | N (not for renovation-only)          | N                       |
| CSOK Plusz          | Y (+ expansion)   | N (not for modernization)            | N                       |
| Babaváró            | Y                 | Y                                    | Y (personal loan)       |
| Falusi CSOK grant   | Y (used: max 50%) | Y (used: the other 50%)              | N                       |
| Falusi CSOK-kölcsön | Y                 | Y (?) (follows the grant's purposes) | N                       |
| Market housing loan | Y                 | Y                                    | N                       |
| Own funds           | Y                 | Y                                    | Y                       |

The calculator therefore pays renovation from own funds, Babaváró, the Falusi CSOK modernization part or a bank loan, never from Otthon Start or CSOK Plusz. Only Babaváró (and own funds) may be kept and invested.

## MNB macroprudential rules (adósságfék)

Legal basis: 32/2014. (IX. 10.) MNB rendelet, amended (last relevant: 3/2026. (II. 26.) MNB rendelet, effective 2026-02-27, applying HFM fully to loans secured by építményi jog).

JTM (jövedelemarányos törlesztőrészlet-mutató), HUF loans, from 2026-01-01:

| Loan / fixed-rate period                             | Net income under 800,000 HUF | Net income 800,000 HUF or more |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Mortgage, rate period under 5 years                  | 25%                          | 30%                            |
| Mortgage, rate period 5-10 years                     | 35%                          | 40%                            |
| Mortgage, rate period 10+ years or fixed to maturity | 50%                          | 60%                            |
| Green-purpose mortgage, 10+ years fixed              | 60%                          | 60%                            |
| Unsecured / short loans                              | 50%                          | 60%                            |
| EUR loans (reference)                                | 25%                          | 30%                            |

- Income threshold was 600,000 HUF until 2025-12-31; 800,000 HUF from 2026-01-01.
- A second threshold listed by MNB rose from 450,000 HUF to 550,000 HUF on 2026-01-01 (role: de minimis / small-loan exemption ?).
- Otthon Start and CSOK Plusz (fixed for the full term) fall under the 50% / 60% row. All existing debt (incl. Babaváró and Munkáshitel installments) counts.

HFM (hitelfedezeti mutató = LTV):

- HUF mortgage: 80%.
- First-time buyers: 90% (since 2024-01-01) - this enables the 10% önerő in Otthon Start and CSOK Plusz.
- Green-purpose: higher limits available.
- EUR mortgage 50%, other FX 35%; car loans 75% (reference).
